## CORE CONTRIBUTIONS TO YOUR DISSERTATION

### 1. **Evolutionary Mismatch Hypothesis**
Larsen provides the **most comprehensive mismatch framework** in evolutionary psychology literature:
- **Parameter Exceeding Hypothesis:** Modern contexts (mating pool size 30,000, OSR 0.20, inequality 100x) exceed evolved system parameters (50-150 pool, OSR 0.95, inequality 2-5x).
- **Temporal Mismatch:** Apps emerged 0→100% in 10 years (too rapid for facultative calibration).
- **Cascading Failure Model:** Positive feedback loops amplify dysfunction (virtual OSR → selectivity spike → inequality → withdrawal → demographic collapse → reinforcement).

### 2. **Historical Validation**
**First scholar to provide empirical evidence that cultural interventions CAN override evolved hypergamy:**
- **First Sexual Revolution (12th century):** Church-enforced monogamy broke polygynous kinship groups → universal male mating access → prosperity + population boom.
- **Second Sexual Revolution (1750s):** Free partner choice without contraception/welfare → illegitimacy crisis (40% in cities) → Victorian backlash.
- **Lesson:** Modern mating crisis has **historical precedents**, and interventions have **worked before**.

### 3. **Demographic Urgency Framing**
Repositions mating crisis from **individual suffering** (loneliness, celibacy) to **civilizational existential threat** (population collapse):
- Norway: 1.4 fertility (-33% per generation)
- South Korea: 0.7 fertility (-65% per generation)
- U.S.: 30% male celibacy (18-30 age group)
- Norway: 33% singlehood rate (+10 percentage points since 1980s)

### 4. **Virtual OSR Hypothesis**
**Revolutionary extension of Stone et al. (2007) sex ratio theory:**
- Traditional OSR = demographic ratio (males:females in population)
- **Virtual OSR** = attention-based ratio (males:females in *actual mating access*)
- **Example:** Tinder physical OSR = 3:1 (male-biased), but women match only top 5% → **effective OSR = 0.2 for average men** (20:1 male competition per available woman)
- **Implication:** Stone's framework (tested OSR 0.90-1.10) **cannot predict** app dynamics (OSR 0.20-5.0)

### 5. **Welfare State Paradox**
**First to argue social democracies inadvertently destroyed mating markets:**
- Norwegian women receive .2M more in lifetime benefits than they pay in taxes.
- State replaces husband as provider → women no longer economically dependent on men.
- **Result:** Women revert to **pure promiscuous selection** (looks, genes, status) because provisioning is guaranteed, not scarce.
- **Prediction:** Most generous welfare states (Norway, Sweden) should have **lowest fertility** (confirmed: Norway 1.4, Sweden 1.5).

### 6. **Radical Policy Proposals**
Only evolutionary psychologist proposing **controversial but testable interventions** in peer-reviewed publications:
- **Socialized Reproduction:** State fully funds single motherhood (housing, childcare, income).
- **Artificial Wombs:** Democratize male reproductive autonomy (currently women are gatekeepers).
- **AI Matchmaking:** Override visual filters, force compatibility-based pairing ("algorithmic arranged marriages").
- **Polygyny Legalization:** Formalize de facto app-based polygyny (top men already have serial access to multiple women).

### **Sexual Inequality (The New Gini Coefficient)**

- **Bottom 30% men:** 0 partners (involuntary celibacy)

- **The 30,000 Swipe Problem:** Median man on Tinder must swipe 30,000 profiles for ONE mating opportunity
- **Female Filtration Cascade:** 95% deleted (looks) → 98% deleted (chat) → 80% deleted (meeting) = **0.01% acceptance rate**

- **Top 20% Rule:** Top 20% of men (by attractiveness) receive 80% of female right-swipes (Pareto distribution)

## CRITIQUES AND LIMITATIONS

### **Methodological Concerns**
1. **Literary Analysis:** Uses novels/sagas as data for historical "Master Narratives." Critique: Elite perspectives, not mass behavior. Response: Pre-survey-era requires proxy measures.
2. **Norway Generalizability:** Most data from homogeneous, small, wealthy Norway. Critique: May not apply to diverse, large, unequal U.S. Response: Trends replicate across all OECD nations.
3. **Causal Inference:** Correlational data (singlehood ↑, app use ↑). Critique: Direction of causality unclear (do apps cause celibacy, or do celibate men use apps more?). Response: Longitudinal studies needed.

### **Theoretical Debates**
1. **Polygyny Proposal:** Feminist critique (patriarchal), egalitarian critique (sacrifices 80% of men). Larsen's response: "I'm predicting, not advocating—hypergamy is unsuppressible in free markets."
2. **Artificial Wombs:** Critique: Commodifies reproduction, eliminates female bodily autonomy benefits. Larsen's response: "Men currently have zero reproductive autonomy (women are gatekeepers). This creates equality."
3. **Determinism:** Critique: Overemphasizes biological imperatives, underplays cultural plasticity. Response: First Sexual Revolution proves culture CAN override biology (monogamy enforcement worked).

### **Empirical Gaps**
Larsen does NOT provide:
- Longitudinal data (is singlehood temporary or permanent?)
- Causal evidence (controlled experiments on app effects)
- Cross-cultural variation in mismatch severity (why Israel 3.0 fertility despite modernity?)

**Your Dissertation Opportunity:** Fill these gaps with Bertrand (causal), Stone (cross-state variation), Schmitt (cross-cultural).
