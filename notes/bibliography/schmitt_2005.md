## CORE CONTRIBUTIONS TO YOUR DISSERTATION

### 1. **Cross-Cultural Validation of Sexual Strategies Theory (SST)**

**Finding:**
Sex differences in sociosexuality (SOI) are **statistically significant in ALL 48 nations** without exception.

**Effect Sizes:**
- **Overall:** d = 0.74 (medium-to-large)
- **Range:** d = 0.30 (Latvia, smallest) to d = 1.24 (Morocco, Ukraine, largest)
- **Variance Explained:** Biological sex η² = 0.15 (15% of variance); Nation η² = 0.06 (6%) → **Biology 2.5x stronger than culture**

**Implication:**
- **Validates Buss & Schmitt (1993) SST cross-culturally** - sex differences in mating strategies are **universal**, not Western artifact
- **Refutes Social Structural Theory** (Eagly & Wood, 1999) - culture modulates but doesn't override evolved sex differences

### 2. **Cross-Cultural Validation of Strategic Pluralism Theory (SPT)**

**Findings:**
1. **Pathogen Prevalence ↔ SOI:** Negative correlation (harsh reproductive environments → restricted SOI)
   - High infant mortality, malnutrition → need biparental investment → monogamy
   - Examples: Bangladesh (high pathogen, SOI 19.67) vs. Finland (low pathogen, SOI 50.50)

2. **Sex Ratio ↔ SOI:** r(46) = **-0.45, p < .001**
   - Female-biased OSR (more women) → unrestricted SOI (women compete via sexual access)
   - Examples: Latvia (OSR 0.85, SOI 46.10) vs. Taiwan (OSR 1.08, SOI 19.22)

3. **Economic Development ↔ SOI:** HDI positively predicts SOI (wealth → unrestricted)
   - Mechanism: Welfare states reduce need for male provisioning → women prioritize attractiveness

**Implication:**
- **Validates Gangestad & Simpson (2000) SPT cross-culturally** - ecological variation predicts SOI as theorized

### 6. **Gender Equality Paradox (Social Structural Theory Debate)**

**Social Structural Theory (Eagly & Wood, 1999) Prediction:**
Greater gender equality → smaller sex differences in mate preferences (cultural roles drive differences)

**Schmitt (2005) Finding:**
- **Correlation:** Gender Empowerment Measure ↔ SOI sex difference magnitude = r = **-0.56*** (p < .001)
- **Interpretation (Social Structural):** Equality → convergence on same preferences

**YOUR Reinterpretation (Larsen 2024 + Schmitt data):**
- Smaller sex differences NOT because **convergence on same preferences**
- But because **both sexes shift toward unrestricted** for **different evolutionary reasons**:
  - **Men:** Exploit abundance (apps give top men unlimited access)
  - **Women:** Express uncompromised hypergamy (economic independence → prioritize attractiveness over provisioning)
- **Result:** **Behavioral convergence** (both promiscuous) but **underlying mechanisms differ** (men opportunistic, women selective)
- **Outcome:** Mating market gridlock (women filter 95% of men, men withdraw/exploit) → demographic collapse (fertility 1.3-1.5)

## CRITIQUES AND LIMITATIONS

### **Self-Report Bias:**
- **Critique:** SOI is self-reported, subject to social desirability (especially in conservative cultures)
- **Response:** Bogus pipeline studies show minimal bias (Alexander & Fisher, 2003); SOI validates against external indices (mate poaching, STI rates)

### **Cultural Sampling:**
- **Critique:** 48 nations, but some regions overrepresented (Europe: 23 nations) vs. underrepresented (Africa: 5 nations)
- **Response:** Captures 70%+ of world's population; key ecological variation (OSR, pathogen, HDI) represented

### **Cross-Sectional Design:**
- **Critique:** Cannot infer causation (e.g., does OSR cause SOI, or vice versa?)
- **Response:** Longitudinal studies (post-2005) confirm directionality (sex ratio shifts predict SOI changes over time)

### **Generational Changes:**
- **Critique:** 2005 data, pre-dating smartphone dating apps (Tinder launched 2012)
- **YOUR Response:** Makes Schmitt even more valuable - provides **pre-app baseline** for comparison. Larsen (2024) shows Nordic countries **now have fertility collapse** (1.3-1.5) despite Schmitt's (2005) high SOI → validates **temporal progression of crisis**
