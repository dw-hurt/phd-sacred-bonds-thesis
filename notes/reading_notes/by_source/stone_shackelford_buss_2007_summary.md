# Stone, Shackelford & Buss (2007) - Analysis Summary

**Citation:** Stone, E. A., Shackelford, T. K., & Buss, D. M. (2007). Sex ratio and mate preferences: A cross-cultural investigation. *European Journal of Social Psychology*, 37(2), 288-296.

**Processing Date:** 2025-12-11

## Key Findings

### Men's Results
- Correlation with sex ratio: r = -0.18 (p < 0.001)
- All 15 attributes negative
- Classical Hypothesis supported

### Women's Results  
- Correlation: r = -0.14 (p < 0.05)
- 12/15 attributes negative
- Financial prospects: r = -0.35 (strongest)
- Chastity: r = +0.29 (anomaly)

### Sample
- 36 cultures, 9,809 participants
- Sex ratio: 0.83-1.14
