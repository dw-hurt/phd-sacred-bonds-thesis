# Buss (1989) - Cross-Reference Integration

**Source:** Buss, D. M. (1989). Sex differences in human mate preferences: Evolutionary hypotheses tested in 37 cultures.

**Integration Date:** 2025-12-11

## Integration Matrix

### Buss (1989) ↔ Trivers (1972)
**Relationship:** Empirical Validation of Theory

**Trivers Predicts:**
- Higher investor (women) value resources
- Lower investor (men) value fertility cues

**Buss Confirms:**
- Women value financial prospects (d = 0.90) in 36/37 cultures
- Men value physical attractiveness (d = -0.81) in all 37 cultures

**Result:** ✅ Trivers validated across 10,047 participants globally

---

### Buss (1989) ↔ Stone et al. (2007)
**Relationship:** Baseline vs. Contextual Flexibility

**Buss:** Universal baseline preferences
- Women: Financial prospects (d = 0.90)
- Men: Physical attractiveness (d = -0.81)

**Stone:** Sex ratio modulates intensity
- Women's resource preference varies: r = -0.35 with sex ratio
- Men's attractiveness preference varies: r = -0.28 with sex ratio

**Integration Strategy (Chapter 3):**
- Section 3.1: Buss establishes universal baseline
- Section 3.5: Stone shows contextual adjustment
- **Conclusion:** Evolved preferences + environmental sensitivity

---

### Buss (1989) ↔ Larsen (2023)
**Relationship:** Individual Preferences vs. Mating Systems

**Buss:** What individuals want in mates
**Larsen:** What mating systems societies accept

**Complementary Evidence:**
- Buss: Women value resources → hypergamy
- Larsen: High sex ratio → polygyny acceptance
- **Together:** Individual + societal levels both respond to ecology

---

### Buss (1989) ↔ Bertrand et al.
**Relationship:** Universal Psychology + Economic Structure

**Buss:** Women universally prefer resourceful men
**Bertrand:** Economic inequality shapes who has resources

**Chapter 4 Integration:**
- Buss explains WHY women are hypergamous (evolved preferences)
- Bertrand explains HOW economics structures hypergamy
- **Together:** Psychology + economics create modern patterns
