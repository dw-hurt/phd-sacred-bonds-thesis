## Integration Matrix

### Trivers (1972) ↔ Stone et al. (2007)
**Relationship:** Theory → Empirical Test

**Trivers:** "Higher investor more selective"
**Stone:** Women (higher investors) more selective when men scarce (r = -0.14)
**Result:** ✅ Theory validated across 36 cultures

**Chapter Usage:**
- Chapter 2: Trivers as theoretical foundation
- Chapter 7: Stone et al. as empirical confirmation

### Trivers (1972) ↔ Buss (1989, 2023)
**Relationship:** Theory → Cross-Cultural Evidence

**Trivers:** Predicts sex differences in mate preferences
**Buss:** Documents preferences across 37 cultures
- Women value resources/status
- Men value youth/beauty
**Result:** ✅ Cross-cultural validation
