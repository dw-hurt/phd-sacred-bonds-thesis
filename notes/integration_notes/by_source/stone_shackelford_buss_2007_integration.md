# Stone, Shackelford & Buss (2007) - Cross-Reference Integration

**Source:** Stone, E. A., Shackelford, T. K., & Buss, D. M. (2007). Sex ratio and mate preferences: A cross-cultural investigation. *European Journal of Social Psychology*, 37(2), 288-296.

**Integration Date:** 2025-12-11

---

## Integration Matrix

### 1. Stone et al. (2007) ↔ Larsen (2023)
**Relationship:** Complementary - Both examine sex ratio effects

| Dimension | Larsen (2023) | Stone et al. (2007) |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Outcome | Polygynous mindset | Mate preference standards |
| Evidence | ~300 participants | 9,809 participants, 36 cultures |
| Chapter | 7 | 7 (primary) |

**Key Integration:**
- Larsen: Sex ratio affects mating SYSTEM acceptance (polygyny)
- Stone: Sex ratio affects mate CHOICE criteria (preferences)
- **Together:** Multi-level sex ratio effects on mating psychology

**Chapter 7 Usage:**
- Section 7.5 - Combine both studies to show sex ratio influences ideology AND preferences

### 2. Stone et al. (2007) ↔ Trivers (1972)
**Relationship:** Foundational - Trivers provides theory, Stone provides test

| Dimension | Trivers (1972) | Stone et al. (2007) |
|-----------|----------------|---------------------|
| Level | Theory | Empirical test |
| Prediction | Higher investor more selective | Women more selective when men scarce |
| Evidence | Cross-species | Human, 36 cultures |

**Key Integration:**
- Trivers: "Greater parental investment → greater selectivity"
- Stone: Women (higher investors) become MORE selective in male-scarce environments (r = -0.14)
- **Validation:** Stone empirically confirms Trivers in humans

**Chapter 2 Usage:**
- Section 2.2 - Use Stone as empirical validation of Trivers's theory

### 3. Stone et al. (2007) ↔ Gangestad & Simpson (2000)
**Relationship:** Theory Test - Stone tests Strategic Pluralism predictions

| Dimension | Gangestad & Simpson (2000) | Stone et al. (2007) |
|-----------|----------------------------|---------------------|
| Level | Theory | Empirical test |
| Hypothesis | Alternative Hypothesis | Alternative Hypothesis supported |
| Focus | Context-dependent strategies | Sex ratio as context |

**Key Integration:**
- G&S: Strategies facultatively adjust to ecological conditions
- Stone: Sex ratio serves as key ecological trigger
- **Result:** Alternative Hypothesis supported for women

**Chapter 3 Usage:**
- Section 3.5 - Use Stone as primary empirical test of Strategic Pluralism

### 4. Stone et al. (2007) ↔ Bertrand et al.

### 5. Stone et al. (2007) ↔ Buss (1989, 2023)
**Relationship:** Baseline vs. Flexibility

| Dimension | Buss | Stone et al. (2007) |
|-----------|------|---------------------|
| Focus | Universal sex differences | Contextual variation |
| Finding | Fixed preferences | Preferences adjust to sex ratio |

**Key Integration:**
- Buss: Baseline universal preferences (men value beauty, women value resources)
