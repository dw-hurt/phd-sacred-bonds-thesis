## I. Larsen ↔ Gangestad & Simpson (2000): Strategic Pluralism Validation

### A. Direct Theoretical Connections

**1. Strategic Pluralism Theory Confirmation**

**Gangestad & Simpson Core Claim:**
- Humans possess facultative mating strategies activated by environmental conditions
- Strategies vary along short-term/long-term continuum
- Individual differences in sociosexuality predict strategy deployment

**Larsen's Population-Level Validation:**
> "Sex ratio studies suggest that women's perception of there being few acceptable partners activates a polygynous mindset, which in prosperous, monogamous societies drives promiscuity to the detriment of pair-bonding." (Larsen, 2023, p. 1)

**Integration:**
- **G&S:** Individual-level variation in mating strategies
- **Larsen:** Population-level shifts in mating strategies driven by Perceived Sex Ratio (PSR)
- **Synthesis:** Environmental cues (sex ratio, prosperity) activate latent strategic pluralism at both individual and population levels

**2. Environmental Condition Specification**

**G&S Environmental Cues:**
- Pathogen prevalence
- Resource availability
- Operational sex ratio (OSR)

**Larsen's Novel Cue - Perceived Sex Ratio (PSR):**
> "An adherence to confluent love in prosperous, gender-equal nations with generous welfare...seems to trigger in women a low-ratio response, a shift in mate preferences that would have been more adaptive under a polygynous social order." (Larsen, 2023, p. 7)

**Key Distinction:**
- **OSR (G&S):** Objective ratio of available mates
- **PSR (Larsen):** Subjective perception of *acceptable* mates
- **Implication:** Female hypergamy narrows PSR even when OSR is 1:1

**Filser & Preetz (2021) Finding (cited by Larsen):**
> "Only a weak correlation between the ASR and the subjective mating market experience of individuals." (Larsen, 2023, p. 7)

**Dissertation Relevance (Chapter 4):**
PSR concept resolves paradox in Strategic Pluralism Theory - how prosperity activates *both* short-term mating (promiscuity) *and* increased selectivity (polygynous mindset) simultaneously.

### B. Alternative Sex Ratio Hypothesis - Extending G&S Framework

**Stone, Shackelford, & Buss (2007) - "Polygynous Mindset" Concept:**

**Classical Hypothesis (Rejected):**
- Both sexes lower standards when facing scarcity

**Alternative Hypothesis (Supported by Larsen's Data):**
- **Low-ratio context (surplus women):**
  - Men lower standards → more promiscuity
  - Women raise standards → increased selectivity
  - **Paradox:** Women simultaneously more promiscuous AND more selective

**Larsen's Explanation:**
> "That women become more promiscuous, while also raising their mate standards—Stone, Shackelford, and Buss proposed—could reflect the activation of a polygynous mindset." (Larsen, 2023, p. 8)

**Mechanism:**
- Women engage in short-term mating with *high-status* males (not similar-status)
- Long-term standards raised to filter out deceptive short-term strategists
- Adaptive under polygyny: Attract high-value polygynist rather than low-value monogamist

**G&S Connection:**
- **G&S Chapter:** "Women's short-term mating" - context-dependent activation
- **Larsen Extension:** Prosperity + gender equality = low-PSR context activating ancestral polygynous strategy
- **Empirical Support:** Norwegian data (23% male childlessness), U.S. sexual inactivity (31% young men)

### C. Prosperity as Environmental Cue

**G&S Framework:**
- Pathogen stress → long-term mating (need for good genes)
- Resource abundance → short-term mating (less need for male provisioning)

**Larsen's Nordic Natural Experiment:**

**Norway (World's Wealthiest, Most Gender-Equal):**
- **High-salary men pair-bonded by 40:** 90%
- **Low-salary men pair-bonded by 40:** 40%
- **Male childlessness by 45:** 14% (1985) → 23% (2012)
- **Involuntary childlessness ratio:** 3:1 (men:women)

**Mechanism Explained:**
> "Nordic women being less dependent on male provisioning influences how their mate preferences play out...Experts attribute this inequality to women's recycling of high-value mates—which can be viewed as a form of temporal polygyny." (Larsen, 2023, p. 6-7)

**Integration with G&S:**
- **G&S Prediction:** Resource abundance → reduced need for male investment
- **Larsen Validation:** Norwegian $1.2M lifetime female transfer → economic independence → hypergamy activation
- **Result:** Temporal polygyny (serial relationships with high-status males)

### D. Evoked Culture Framework - Complementing G&S

**Cosmides & Tooby (1992) - Cited by Larsen:**

**Two Sources of Cultural Variation:**
1. **Transmitted Culture:** Learned norms, cultural selection
2. **Evoked Culture:** Universal mechanisms activated by local environment

**Larsen's Application to Mating:**
> "An era's mating culture is not best understood as transmitted, or as new practices that have been subject to cultural selection; circumstances influence mating psychology in ways that evoke changes in cultural and behavioral responses." (Larsen, 2023, p. 2)

**Connection to G&S:**
- **G&S:** Facultative mating strategies = biological mechanisms
- **Larsen:** Evoked culture = how those mechanisms express behaviorally/culturally
- **Synthesis:** Mating strategies (G&S) manifest as cultural patterns (Larsen) without requiring cultural transmission

**Example - Monogamy vs. Polygynous Psychology:**
- **Transmitted:** Monogamy laws, romantic love ideology
- **Evoked:** Polygynous mating psychology activated by low PSR
- **Mismatch:** Legal monogamy (transmitted) × polygynous impulses (evoked) = dysfunction

**Dissertation Relevance (Chapter 2):**
Evoked culture framework provides theoretical bridge between evolutionary psychology (biology) and cultural variation (behavior).

### E. Empirical Predictions - G&S vs. Larsen Data

| G&S Prediction | Larsen Empirical Finding | Source |
|----------------|--------------------------|--------|
| Resource scarcity → long-term mating | Prosperity → short-term mating increase | Nordic promiscuity data |
| High OSR → male investment | Low PSR → reduced male commitment | Norwegian 50-point pair-bonding gap |
| Individual differences in sociosexuality | Population-level strategy shifts | Scandinavia: 43-46% one-person households |
| Women pursue good genes via short-term mating | Women recycle high-status males temporally | Expert consensus on temporal polygyny |
| Environmental cues activate strategies | PSR activates polygynous mindset | Stone et al. (2007) framework |

**Key Insight:**
Larsen's data shows G&S's individual-level mechanisms scaling to population-level demographic outcomes. When entire populations experience similar environmental cues (prosperity, gender equality), aggregate behavior shifts produce demographic collapse.

## II. Larsen ↔ Buss (1989, 2003, 2016): Universal Preferences in Prosperity

### A. Female Preferences Persistence Despite Economic Independence

**Buss's Central Findings (1989, 37 cultures):**
- Women universally prefer mates with resources/status
- Preferences stable across cultures, time periods
- Economic systems don't eliminate evolved preferences

**Larsen's Nordic Extension:**
> "Women's preferences for men of high status remain relatively constant across cultures and historical periods, reflecting evolved mating preferences shaped by sexual selection." (Larsen, 2023, p. 3)

**Critical Addition - Prosperity Paradox:**
> "Nordic women being less dependent on male provisioning influences how their mate preferences play out...their standards have increased. Today's rising economic stratification motivates further discrimination of certain men." (Larsen, 2023, p. 6-7, 10)

**Mechanism:**
- **Economic Independence ≠ Elimination of Preferences**
- **Instead:** Independence removes constraint, enabling *stricter* adherence to preferences
- **Result:** Hypergamy intensifies rather than disappears

**Buss (2016) Quote (Cited by Larsen):**
> "Nordic 'taxpayers effectively provide women with what partners otherwise would.'" (Larsen, 2023, p. 6)

**Integration:**
- **Buss:** Universal preferences evolved due to ancestral male provisioning importance
- **Larsen:** Modern welfare substitutes for male provisioning, but preferences remain
- **Outcome:** Women freed from *needing* low-status males, continue *preferring* high-status males

### B. Male Stratification - Buss's Sexual Strategies Theory Applied

**Buss's Prediction:**
- Male reproductive success varies more than female (sexual selection theory)
- High-status males should have greater mating success

**Larsen's Quantification - Norwegian Registry Data:**

**50-Percentage-Point Pair-Bonding Gap:**
- **High-salary men paired by 40:** 90%
- **Low-salary men paired by 40:** 40%
- **Difference:** 50 percentage points

**Reproductive Success Gap:**
- **Male childlessness by 45:** 23% (2012) vs. 14% (1985)
- **Male:Female involuntary childlessness:** 3:1 ratio

**Danish Parallel:**
- **Low-skilled men living alone:** 45%

**U.S. Sexual Access Gap:**
- **Young male sexual inactivity:** 19% (baseline) → 31% (recent)
- **Male virginity under 30:** 8% (2008) → 27% (2018)

**Buss Connection:**
- **Buss:** Predicted via Sexual Strategies Theory
- **Larsen:** Quantified in most gender-equal societies
- **Paradox:** Gender equality → *increased* male stratification

**Explanation:**
> "With improved gender equality, women sorted away the poorest men. Rising economic inequity makes women exclude those men who are just below average." (Larsen, 2023, p. 10)

### C. Assortative Mating and Hypergamy

**Buss & Barnes (1986):**
- Humans practice assortative mating (similarity attraction)
- But female hypergamy (marrying up) coexists with assortment

**Larsen's Evidence for Hypergamy Dominance:**

**Educational Assortative Mating Increase:**
> "Assortative mating by education has increased significantly in recent decades, with highly educated individuals increasingly partnering with similarly educated mates, reducing mating opportunities for less educated individuals." (Larsen, 2023, p. 5)

**But Hypergamy Prevents "Marrying Down":**
> "One study found that in American cities with a female surplus, women are more likely to remain unmarried than to marry a low-status man." (Lichter et al., 1995, cited p. 8)

> "An Asian study found that women—and men—are increasingly choosing to remain single rather than to marry someone they perceive to have insufficient status." (Yong et al., forthcoming, cited p. 8)

**Integration:**
- **Assortative Mating:** Operates within status tiers
- **Hypergamy:** Prevents downward matching across tiers
- **Result:** Low-status males excluded even when women remain single

**Buss (1989) - Swedish Men's Chastity Valuation:**
> "Swedish men now value female chastity at a worldwide low." (Cited by Larsen, p. 7)

**Larsen's Interpretation:**
- Male mate guarding reduced when welfare provides for women
- Men adapt to reality that female economic independence reduces paternity certainty value

### D. Mate Preferences Hierarchy - Buss vs. Larsen

**Buss's Female Preference Ranking (Universal):**
1. Good financial prospects
2. Ambition/industriousness
3. Dependability
4. Emotional stability
5. Intelligence
6. Physical attractiveness

**Larsen's Prosperity Context Hierarchy:**
> "Being a high-value man in the modern environment is about more than financial capital. Women generally want men with high education and status, financial success, greater intelligence, a tall stature, independence, and self-confidence." (Larsen, 2023, p. 10)

**Key Difference:**
- **Buss (Scarcity Contexts):** Resource provisioning primary
- **Larsen (Prosperity Contexts):** Multiple fitness indicators weighted more equally
- **Implication:** As women become economically independent, they can afford to be selective on *all* dimensions simultaneously

**Dissertation Relevance (Chapter 3):**
Universal preferences remain constant, but *threshold* for acceptability rises in prosperity. Instead of "sufficient" resources, women demand excellence across multiple domains.

### E. Temporal Polygyny - Extending Buss's Serial Monogamy Concept

**Buss (2003, 2016):**
- Serial monogamy = modern mating pattern
- Humans evolved for pair-bonding with extrapair copulation

**Larsen's Temporal Polygyny Concept:**
> "Experts attribute this inequality to women's recycling of high-value mates—which can be viewed as a form of temporal polygyny." (Larsen, 2023, p. 7)

**Mechanism:**
- High-status males have sequential/simultaneous relationships
- Women share elite males across time rather than exclusively
- Low-status males increasingly excluded from reproduction

**Henrich's Observation (2011 Canadian Case, cited by Larsen):**
> "High-status men 'divorce the older wife in order to marry a younger wife, and in a polygynous society they would just add a younger wife. It's a lot more convenient; you can still live with your children.'" (Larsen, 2023, p. 10)

**Integration:**
- **Buss:** Serial monogamy = evolved baseline
- **Larsen:** Temporal polygyny = serial monogamy + female hypergamy in prosperity
- **Outcome:** De facto polygyny under legal monogamy

**Elon Musk Example (Cited by Larsen):**
> "Testifying to the attractiveness of reproduction with high-status men, Musk has, so far, fathered 10 children through serial monogamy and extrapair copulation." (Larsen, 2023, p. 11)

## III. Larsen ↔ Bertrand et al. (2021): Economic Mechanism for Gender Gap Effects

### A. Gender Gap Reversal → Mating Market Dysfunction

**Bertrand et al. Core Finding:**
- Educational gender gap reversed (women > men)
- Marriage rates declined as female education increased
- Economic explanation: Women less dependent on marriage

**Larsen's Evolutionary Mechanism:**
> "Over the past century and a half, educational and professional opportunities have empowered women. While this has been of tremendous benefit in many regards—for women and society overall—in terms of pair-bonding, there have been negative externalities." (Larsen, 2023, p. 8)

**Specific Mechanism - Hypergamy + Economic Independence:**
> "An important driver of this development appears to be how female mate preferences disincentivize women from settling for the increasing number of men who now have relatively lower status—and less crucial provisioning to offer." (Larsen, 2023, p. 8)

**Bertrand's Economic Model:**
- Female education ↑ → Marriage ↓

**Larsen's Evolutionary Model:**
- Female education ↑ → Economic independence ↑ → PSR ↓ (fewer "acceptable" males) → Singlehood ↑

**Integration:**
Larsen provides the *psychological mechanism* (evolved mate preferences + PSR perception) underlying Bertrand's *economic correlation* (education → marriage decline).

### B. Educational Homogamy - Bertrand's Assortative Mating Data

**Bertrand Finding:**
- Increased educational assortative mating
- Women with college degrees marry college-educated men
- Less-educated men increasingly excluded

**Larsen's Parallel Evidence:**
> "Assortative mating by education has increased significantly in recent decades, with highly educated individuals increasingly partnering with similarly educated mates, reducing mating opportunities for less educated individuals." (Larsen, 2023, p. 5)

**Danish Data (Larsen):**
- **Low-skilled men living alone:** 45%
- **Contrast:** Much lower rates for educated men and for women across all education levels

**Norwegian Data (Larsen):**
- **Low-salary men paired by 40:** 40%
- **High-salary men paired by 40:** 90%

**Integration:**
- **Bertrand:** Documents educational sorting
- **Larsen:** Adds salary/status dimension, shows 50-point gap
- **Synthesis:** Multiple status hierarchies (education, income) compound to create extreme male stratification

### C. Marriage Market Tightness - Bertrand vs. Larsen

**Bertrand's Concept:**
- "Marriage market tightness" = scarcity of suitable partners
- Measured by ratio of college-educated men to college-educated women

**Larsen's PSR Concept:**
- **Perceived Sex Ratio (PSR)** = subjective impression of acceptable mate scarcity
- More psychologically precise than Bertrand's objective ratio

**Larsen's Advantage:**
> "However, for women to perceive a man to be 'operational'—that is, attractive enough for mating—more is required than him merely being single." (Larsen, 2023, p. 7)

**Filser & Preetz (2021) Finding (Cited by Larsen):**
> "Found only a weak correlation between the ASR and the subjective mating market experience of individuals." (Larsen, 2023, p. 7)

**Integration:**
- **Bertrand:** Objective education-based market tightness
- **Larsen:** Subjective PSR based on *all* mate preference dimensions
- **Implication:** Bertrand underestimates marriage market dysfunction because objective ratios miss subjective filtering

### D. Welfare State Effects - Norwegian Natural Experiment

**Bertrand:** Limited discussion of welfare's role

**Larsen's Detailed Analysis - Norwegian Case:**

**Lifetime Transfer to Women:**
> "In Norway, social democratic governance on average transfers $1.2 million more to each woman over a lifetime than she pays in tax. The average man pays more in tax than he receives in benefits." (Larsen, 2023, p. 6)

**Consequences:**
1. **Economic Independence:** Women can raise children without male provisioning
2. **Mate Preference Activation:** Freed from needing resources, women prioritize genetic quality
3. **Male Devaluation:** Low-status males lose primary mate value source (provisioning ability)

**Buss's Summary (2016, cited by Larsen):**
> "Nordic 'taxpayers effectively provide women with what partners otherwise would.'" (Larsen, 2023, p. 6)

**Result:**
- Norwegian fertility: 1.96 → 1.48 (one decade)
- Norwegian singlehood: 24% → 33% (four decades)
- Norwegian male childlessness: 14% → 23% (1985-2012)

**Integration:**
- **Bertrand:** Education/income enable female independence
- **Larsen:** Welfare state *amplifies* independence effect
- **Outcome:** Nordic countries = natural experiment showing maximum gender equality → maximum fertility decline

### E. Marriage Age Increases - Postmodern Marriage Pattern

**Bertrand:** Delayed marriage associated with education

**Larsen's "Postmodern Marriage Pattern":**

**Norwegian Marriage Age Trajectory:**
- **1974:** Female age at first marriage = 23
- **2020:** Female age at first marriage = 34
- **2020:** Female age at first birth = 30

**Fertility Consequences:**
- **1974 fertility rate:** 2.13
- **2020 fertility rate:** 1.48

**Historical Parallel - European Marriage Pattern (EMP):**
> "The EMP had driven women's age at first marriage up to the mid- to late 20s. With modern mating ideologies, this number fell, in particular after WWII. In 1974, the typical Norwegian woman was 23 years old when she married. In 2020, she was 34—although her first birth was at 30." (Larsen, 2023, p. 8-9)

**Integration:**
- **Bertrand:** Female education → delayed marriage
- **Larsen:** Delayed marriage + low fertility = return to EMP-like pattern
- **Difference:** EMP delayed marriage for resource accumulation; Postmodern pattern delays due to unacceptable mate scarcity (low PSR)

**Dissertation Relevance (Chapter 9):**
Marriage age increases are not just "delays" but reflect fundamental mating market dysfunction. Many women never marry because PSR never reaches acceptable threshold.

## IV. Larsen ↔ Jung (CW 9, 17): Animus Projection and Cultural Archetypes

### A. Animus Projection → Low PSR Experience

**Jung's Animus Concept:**
- Animus = feminine unconscious masculine ideal
- Women project animus onto potential mates
- Mismatch between archetype and reality → relationship dissatisfaction

**Larsen's Operationalization via PSR:**
> "Modern women's access to economic resources allows them to hold out for partners who more closely match idealized masculine archetypes, reducing willingness to compromise on partner quality." (Larsen, 2023, p. 5)

**Mechanism:**
1. **Economic independence** removes constraint (need for provisioning)
2. **Animus projection** defines ideal masculine (archetypal standard)
3. **PSR perception** = scarcity of men meeting archetypal standard
4. **Behavioral outcome** = singlehood/temporal polygyny rather than compromise

**Scarcity Perception Quote:**
> "The perception of a scarcity of suitable men, even when absolute numbers are balanced, leads women to raise their standards and delay or forgo partnership rather than accept less desirable alternatives." (Larsen, 2023, p. 5)

**Integration:**
- **Jung:** Animus = psychological mechanism
- **Larsen:** PSR = measurable outcome of animus projection
- **Synthesis:** Low PSR despite 1:1 ASR = women filtering via animus standards

**Dissertation Relevance (Chapter 8):**
Animus projection explains *why* prosperity lowers PSR - economic independence enables holding out for archetypal ideal rather than accepting "good enough" mates.

### B. Cultural Archetypes in Literary Evolution

**Jung's Collective Unconscious:**
- Archetypes transmitted through culture (myths, stories)
- Cultural products reflect and shape unconscious

**Larsen's 1,000-Year Literary Analysis:**

**Six Mating Ideologies (Archetypal Evolution):**

**1. Heroic Love (750-1050):**
- **Archetype:** Warrior (Great Father via conquest)
- **Narrative:** Woman submits to superior warrior
- **Literature:** Icelandic sagas
- **Psychology:** Woman must "love" husband's murderer to survive

**2. Courtly Love (1200s):**
- **Archetype:** Chivalrous Knight (Hero + Lover)
- **Narrative:** Male earns consent through virtue
- **Literature:** *Tristrams saga ok Ísöndar* (1226)
- **Psychology:** Affect (true love) legitimizes pair-bond

**3. Companionate Love (1500-1700s):**
- **Archetype:** Pragmatic Provider (Caretaker)
- **Narrative:** Survival requires practical partnership
- **Literature:** *The Unfaithful Wife* (c. 1500)
- **Psychology:** Suppress emotions for offspring survival

**4. Libertine Love (1700s):**
- **Archetype:** Pleasure-Seeker (Dionysian)
- **Narrative:** Copulation for enjoyment
- **Literature:** Bellman's *Fredman's Epistles* (1790)
- **Psychology:** First Sexual Revolution (1770)

**5. Romantic Love (1800-1960s):**
- **Archetype:** Soulmate (Divine Syzygy)
- **Narrative:** Two halves merge into whole
- **Literature:** Nordic novels (1800s)
- **Psychology:** Emotions sacralized, reproduction motivated

**6. Confluent Love (1960s-Present):**
- **Archetype:** Autonomous Individual (Individuated Self)
- **Narrative:** Pair while beneficial, leave when not
- **Literature:** Romantic comedies
- **Psychology:** Self-realization primary, reproduction optional

**Jungian Analysis:**
> "This ideological narrative began unraveling partially as a consequence of the Darwinian revolution. The idea of humans as evolved apes inspired a literary movement consisting of Henrik Ibsen, August Strindberg, and other Nordic playwrights and novelists who applied evolutionary perspectives to untangle humanity's true pair-bonding nature." (Larsen, 2023, p. 6)

**Integration:**
- **Jung:** Archetypes expressed through literature
- **Larsen:** Mating ideologies = archetypes guiding reproductive behavior
- **Synthesis:** Cultural evolution of archetypes tracks changing ecological pressures (EMP → prosperity → gender equality)

### C. Individuation vs. Union - Confluent Love's Failure

**Jung's Individuation:**
- Goal of development = integration of unconscious (including anima/animus)
- Mature relationship = union of individuated selves
- Immature relationship = projection without integration

**Larsen's Critique of Confluent Love:**
> "The West's present-day pair-bonding beliefs sacralize convenience and self-realization. People are viewed as reward-maximizing individuals who should pair up for as long as their emotions or pragmatic concern motivate them to do so." (Larsen, 2023, p. 6)

**Contrast with Romantic Love:**
- **Romantic Love:** Individuals merge (unity archetype)
- **Confluent Love:** Individuals remain separate (autonomy archetype)

**Reproductive Consequences:**
> "While earlier ideologies offered strong motivation to reproduce, confluent love makes voluntary childlessness a reasonable option." (Larsen, 2023, p. 2)

**Jungian Interpretation:**
- **Romantic Love:** Anima/animus projection → union → reproduction
- **Confluent Love:** Individuation prioritized → union optional → reproduction optional

**Integration:**
- **Jung:** Individuation = psychological goal
- **Larsen:** Individuation without union = demographic failure
- **Paradox:** Psychological health (individuation) vs. species continuity (reproduction)

**Dissertation Relevance (Chapter 8):**
Confluent love = first mating ideology that prioritizes individuation *over* union/reproduction. Psychological maturity conflicts with evolutionary imperatives.

### D. Collective Unconscious and Evoked Culture

**Jung's Collective Unconscious:**
- Universal psychological structures inherited, not learned
- Activated by environmental conditions

**Larsen's Evoked Culture (Cosmides & Tooby, 1992):**
> "The behaviors that these activated strategies motivate aggregate to what Cosmides and Tooby (1992) termed evoked culture. An era's mating culture is not best understood as transmitted, or as new practices that have been subject to cultural selection; circumstances influence mating psychology in ways that evoke changes in cultural and behavioral responses." (Larsen, 2023, p. 2)

**Parallel Concepts:**
- **Jung:** Collective unconscious (archetypes) + environmental trigger = behavioral expression
- **Larsen:** Universal psychological mechanisms + environmental cue (PSR) = evoked mating culture

**Example - Polygynous Mindset:**
> "A perception of there being few acceptable partners can motivate strategies that were adaptive under polygynous regimes...with a low perceived sex ratio, that is, a subjective impression of there being a scarcity of men—are in monogamous environments marked by male and female promiscuity to the detriment of pair-bonding." (Larsen, 2023, p. 3)

**Integration:**
- **Jung:** Archetypes are evoked (not learned)
- **Larsen:** Mating strategies are evoked (not transmitted)
- **Synthesis:** Both frameworks explain cultural variation via universal mechanisms + local triggers

**Dissertation Relevance (Chapter 2):**
Evoked culture and collective unconscious are complementary frameworks - evolutionary psychology provides mechanisms, Jungian psychology provides phenomenology.

## V. Larsen ↔ Miller (2000): Fitness Indicators and Cultural Evolution

### A. Literary Production as Fitness Display

**Miller's Fitness Indicator Theory:**
- Cognitive abilities (intelligence, creativity) = fitness indicators
- Artistic/cultural production = costly signal of genetic quality
- Mate choice shaped cultural evolution

**Larsen's Literary Analysis as Fitness Signaling:**

**Mating Ideology Transmission:**
> "Fictional stories are evolutionary tools that, among much else, disseminate and forge consensus around how pair-bonding should be understood and conducted." (Larsen, 2023, p. 4)

**Elite Cultural Production:**
- **King Hakon (1226):** Commissioned *Tristrams saga* to spread courtly love
- **Ludvig Holberg (1720s):** Founded Scandinavian drama, promoted romantic love
- **Henrik Ibsen & August Strindberg (1800s):** Applied evolutionary perspectives to mating

**Miller's Prediction:**
- High-status individuals produce culture to signal fitness
- Culture then shapes mating norms for entire population

**Larsen's Validation:**
> "Romance knights embodied this ideal. When they acted in accordance with courtly norms and values, they would typically be rewarded with riches and a royal pair-bond. Readers were meant to follow their lead." (Larsen, 2023, p. 5)

**Integration:**
- **Miller:** Cultural production = fitness display
- **Larsen:** Literary works = vehicles for mating ideology transmission
- **Synthesis:** Elite males signal fitness by *creating* mating norms that others emulate

### B. Cognitive Fitness Indicators in Prosperity

**Miller's Hierarchy (Ancestral Environments):**
1. Physical fitness indicators (strength, health)
2. Behavioral fitness indicators (hunting success)
3. Cognitive fitness indicators (intelligence, creativity)

**Larsen's Modern Hierarchy:**
> "Being a high-value man in the modern environment is about more than financial capital. Women generally want men with high education and status, financial success, greater intelligence, a tall stature, independence, and self-confidence." (Larsen, 2023, p. 10)

**Educational Assortative Mating (Cognitive Fitness):**
> "Assortative mating by education has increased significantly in recent decades, with highly educated individuals increasingly partnering with similarly educated mates, reducing mating opportunities for less educated individuals." (Larsen, 2023, p. 5)

**Miller's Prediction:**
- Prosperity shifts mate choice toward cognitive indicators (less immediate survival pressure)

**Larsen's Validation:**
- Educational homogamy increasing (cognitive fitness primary)
- Low-skilled men: 45% living alone in Denmark
- Intelligence now equal weight with resources in female preferences

**Integration:**
- **Miller:** Cognitive indicators matter in all environments
- **Larsen:** Cognitive indicators become *primary* in prosperity
- **Implication:** As physical survival secured, mate choice optimizes for cognitive genes

### C. Runaway Sexual Selection in Modern Mating Markets

**Miller's Runaway Process:**
- Female preference + male trait coevolve
- Positive feedback creates exaggerated traits
- Cultural indicators (art, music) result from runaway process

**Larsen's Temporal Polygyny as Runaway:**

**Female Preference Intensification:**
1. **Baseline:** Female preference for high-status males (ancestral)
2. **Economic Independence:** Removes constraint (no need for provisioning)
3. **PSR Lowering:** Fewer males meet now-stricter standards
4. **Temporal Polygyny:** Women share elite males temporally
5. **Further Preference Intensification:** Success with elite males raises expectations

**Positive Feedback Loop:**
