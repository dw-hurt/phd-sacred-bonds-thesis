## Key Concepts

### 1. Sacred Union and Hieros Gamos
The symbolic sacred marriage representing union of opposites and psychological/spiritual integration through intimate relationship.

**Relevance to Pair Bonding**:
- Intimate relationship as sacred practice, not merely biological or social
- Union of masculine and feminine principles within and between partners
- Sexual intimacy as potentially spiritual/transpersonal experience
- Partnership as path to transcendence and self-realization

### 2. Numinous Experience in Relationships
Experiences of the numinous (awe, mystery, the divine) within intimate bonding.

**Relevance to Pair Bonding**:
- Moments of profound connection transcend ordinary consciousness
- "Falling in love" often involves numinous, transpersonal experience
- Long-term relationships can maintain access to numinous dimensions
- Loss of numinous quality contributes to relationship dissatisfaction

### 3. Relationship as Spiritual Path
Intimate partnership as vehicle for psychological and spiritual development, not just companionship or reproduction.

**Relevance to Pair Bonding**:
- Relationship challenges as opportunities for growth and awakening
- Partner as mirror for self-knowledge and transformation
- Commitment as spiritual practice requiring consciousness
- Dissolution of ego boundaries in mature, transpersonal love

### 4. Self-Realization Through Union
The potential for actualizing one's fullest capacities and authentic being through intimate relationship.

**Relevance to Pair Bonding**:
- Partner relationship supports or hinders self-actualization
- Healthy bonding enhances both partners' development
- Codependence vs. interdependence in supporting growth
- Balance between union and individuality essential
