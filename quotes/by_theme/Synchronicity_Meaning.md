## Key Concepts

### 1. Jung's Synchronicity Principle
Meaningful coincidences that cannot be explained by causality alone; an acausal connecting principle linking psyche and matter.

**Relevance to Pair Bonding**:
- "Fated" encounters with future partners
- Meaningful coincidences in relationship formation
- Timing of meetings experienced as "meant to be"
- Synchronistic events in relationship development

### 2. Meaningful Encounters and "Destiny"
Experiences of destined or fated meetings with romantic partners that feel guided by forces beyond individual control.

**Relevance to Pair Bonding**:
- Many couples report synchronistic circumstances of first meeting
- Sense of inevitability or "rightness" in relationship formation
- Repeated coincidences bringing potential partners together
- Retrospective narrative of "meant to find each other"

### 3. Archetypal Constellation
How activation of archetypes in the unconscious can correspond with external events, creating synchronistic experiences.

**Relevance to Pair Bonding**:
- Inner psychological readiness corresponds with meeting suitable partner
- Archetypal activation (e.g., anima/animus) coincides with encounter
- Psychological state influences whom one attracts or is drawn to
- Unconscious factors create conditions for synchronistic meeting

### 4. Meaning-Making in Relationships
How partners construct meaningful narratives about their relationship origin and development.

**Relevance to Pair Bonding**:
- Couples create "origin stories" emphasizing meaningful connection
- Synchronistic elements incorporated into relationship narrative
- Meaning attribution strengthens bond and commitment
- Loss of meaning-making capacity correlates with relationship dissolution
