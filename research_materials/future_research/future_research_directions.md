## PRIORITY 1: Essential for PhD Completion

### A. Jungian Psychology and Archetypal Theory ⭐⭐⭐ CRITICAL

**Why Critical:** Your Sacred Bonds framework explicitly integrates Jungian concepts (archetypes, individuation, shadow, anima/animus), but you currently lack primary Jungian sources.

#### Recommended Sources:

**1. Jung, C.G. (1959). *The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious***
- **CW 9, Part 1** - Essential primary source
- **Key concepts:** Anima/Animus, Self, Shadow, individuation
- **Relevance:** Theoretical foundation for archetypal dimensions of mating
- **Priority:** CRITICAL - cite primary sources, not secondary interpretations

**2. Jung, C.G. (1960). *The Structure and Dynamics of the Psyche***
- **CW 8** - Includes synchronicity essay
- **Key concepts:** Synchronicity, psychic energy, complexes
- **Relevance:** Direct support for synchronicity dimension
- **Priority:** CRITICAL

**3. Stevens, A. (1982). *Archetype: A Natural History of the Self***
- **Modern synthesis** of Jung + evolutionary psychology
- Bridges Jungian theory with biological evolution
- **Relevance:** Shows how archetypes emerge from evolutionary history
- **Priority:** HIGH - excellent for integration chapter

**4. Knox, J. (2003). *Archetype, Attachment, Analysis: Jungian Psychology and the Emergent Mind***
- Integrates attachment theory with Jungian psychology
- Neuroscience validation of archetypal patterns
- **Relevance:** Modern scientific grounding for Jung
- **Priority:** HIGH

**5. Hogenson, G.B. (2001). "The Baldwin Effect: A Neglected Influence on C.G. Jung's Evolutionary Thinking"**
- *Journal of Analytical Psychology*
- Connects Jung to modern evolutionary theory
- **Relevance:** Bridges evolutionary biology and archetypal psychology
- **Priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH
