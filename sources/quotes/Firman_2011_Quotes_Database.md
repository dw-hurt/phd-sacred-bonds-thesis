## Usage Notes

### Direct Quotation Guidelines

**When to Use Direct Quotes:**
1. Quantitative findings (77%, 3.3:1) → Always quote verbatim for precision
2. Novel theoretical claims → Quote to attribute properly
3. Particularly elegant phrasing → Quote to preserve author's voice

**When to Paraphrase:**
1. Methodological descriptions → Summarize in your own words
2. Background information → Integrate into narrative
3. Multiple similar points → Synthesize rather than stacking quotes

### Citation Format (Chicago Author-Date)

**Direct Quote:**
> "Males from selected lineages sired 77% of offspring when in sperm competition with males from control lineages" (Firman and Simmons 2011, 4).

**Paraphrase:**
> Experimental evolution demonstrates that genetically superior males achieve 3.3-fold fertilization advantages under polyandrous conditions (Firman and Simmons 2011).

**Integrated Citation:**
> Firman and Simmons (2011) found that males from selected lineages "sired 77% of offspring" in competitive trials, representing a 3.3-fold reproductive advantage.
