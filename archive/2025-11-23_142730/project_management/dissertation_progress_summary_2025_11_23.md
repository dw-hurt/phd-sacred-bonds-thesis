## III. Theoretical Integration - Synthesis Status

### A. Core Theoretical Framework ✅ COMPLETE

**Strategic Pluralism Theory (G&S) + Sex Ratio Theory (Larsen):**
- Facultative strategies activated by environmental cues
- PSR (Perceived Sex Ratio) = key cue in prosperity contexts
- Low PSR → polygynous mindset → dual strategy (promiscuity + high selectivity)
- **Integration Quality:** Excellent (Larsen validates G&S at population level)

**Universal Preferences (Buss) + Evoked Culture (Larsen):**
- Preferences stable across cultures/time
- Prosperity removes constraint (economic independence) → stricter adherence
- Hypergamy persists even in most gender-equal societies
- **Integration Quality:** Excellent (resolves prosperity paradox)

**Economic Mechanisms (Bertrand) + Evolutionary Psychology (All):**
- Bertrand: Economic correlation (education → marriage decline)
- Larsen: Psychological mechanism (independence → PSR lowering → singlehood)
- **Integration Quality:** Excellent (complete causal chain)

**Archetypes (Jung) + Fitness Indicators (Miller) + Literary Evolution (Larsen):**
- Animus projection = psychological mechanism underlying PSR perception
- Cultural archetypes = fitness displays (Miller's framework)
- Literary evolution tracks changing fitness indicators across 1,000 years
- **Integration Quality:** Strong (novel interdisciplinary synthesis)

### B. Evoked vs. Transmitted Culture Framework ✅ CRITICAL INSIGHT

**Larsen's Core Contribution (Cosmides & Tooby, 1992):**
- **Transmitted Culture:** Learned norms (monogamy laws, romantic love ideology)
- **Evoked Culture:** Universal mechanisms activated by environment (polygynous psychology)
- **Mismatch:** Legal monogamy × polygynous impulses = dysfunction

**Integration Power:**
- Resolves nature/culture debate (both operate, different timescales)
- Explains rapid behavioral change without new mutations (1960s Sexual Revolution)
- Predicts policy failure (fighting evoked culture with transmitted norms)

**Dissertation Impact:**
This framework integrates ALL sources:
- G&S: Strategies are evoked, not learned
- Buss: Preferences are evoked, cultural variation is minor
- Jung: Archetypes are evoked (collective unconscious)
- Miller: Fitness displays adapt to evoked preferences
- Bertrand: Economic changes evoke new mating patterns
