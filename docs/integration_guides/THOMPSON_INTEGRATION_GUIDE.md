## 🎯 Key Contributions to Your Thesis

### Why Thompson Matters for Your Dissertation

#### 1. **Fills Critical Technology Gap**
Your thesis examines evolutionary, psychological, demographic, and cultural factors in the contemporary mating crisis. Thompson provides the **technological dimension**—showing how digital platforms mediate, constrain, and shape modern mate selection.

#### 2. **Empirical Evidence for Technology Critique**
Larsen (2023) offers philosophical critique of technology's alienating effects; Thompson provides **concrete empirical data** validating these concerns through OkCupid case study.

#### 3. **Cross-Cultural Mating Diversity Data**
- Only **17% of human cultures** practice strict monogamy
- **21% of U.S. adults** report consensual non-monogamy experience
- Supports evolutionary psychology premise of mating strategy flexibility

#### 4. **Platform Design as Intervention Point**
Shows that **design choices matter**—technology can either support or constrain mating strategy diversity. Suggests **actionable recommendations** for addressing crisis through better platform design.

#### 5. **Alternative Relationship Structures Focus**
Directly relevant to **Chapter 7** (Alternative Relationship Structures). Documents challenges of representing non-monogamous identities in digital spaces and how platforms privilege hierarchical coupledom.

## ⚠️ Important Research Notes

### Sample Limitations
- **Demographics:** Primarily white, middle-class, educated
- **Location:** West Coast U.S.
- **Identity:** CNM and LGBTQ+ focused
- **Platform:** OkCupid only

**How to Address:**
- Note as limitation in your dissertation
- Argue findings are "proof of concept"—if even "inclusive" platforms have issues, mainstream platforms likely worse
- Frame CNM users as "canaries in coal mine" revealing platform problems affecting all users

### Pre-Publication Status
- Paper is "in press" (not yet published)
- DOI not yet assigned
- Final page numbers may differ

**How to Address:**
- Cite as: Thompson, R. (in press)
- Note pre-publication status in bibliography
- Update citation when final version published
